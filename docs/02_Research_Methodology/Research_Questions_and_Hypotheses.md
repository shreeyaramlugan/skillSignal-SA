## 1. Purpose

This document will record the research questions and hypotheses
used to guide SkillSignal SA.

Questions and hypotheses must be measurable using the
available and permitted dataset.

## 2. Research Question Areas

The following areas are derived from the approved project
objectives. They are research question candidates, not
final approved questions.

### RQ1 — Skills Demand

Which technical and professional skills are most frequently
advertised across the seven technology job families?

### RQ2 — Experience Requirements

What experience requirements are advertised across
technology job families and seniority levels?

### RQ3 — Qualifications

What qualifications and educational requirements are
commonly specified in technology job advertisements?

### RQ4 — Junior and Graduate Opportunities

What proportion of sampled advertisements target junior,
entry-level or graduate candidates?

### RQ5 — Geographic Patterns

How does advertised technology employment demand vary
across South African provinces and cities?

### RQ6 — Temporal Patterns

How does advertised technology employment demand change
over the selected 12-month research window?

### RQ7 — Salary Transparency

How frequently do advertisements disclose salary information,
and how does disclosure vary across job families?

### RQ8 — Skill Relationships

Which technical skills are frequently advertised together,
and how do these relationships vary across job families?

## 3. Hypotheses

No inferential hypotheses are approved at this stage.

Potential hypotheses must be developed in Section B with:
- A clear null hypothesis (H0).
- A corresponding alternative hypothesis (H1).
- Defined independent and dependent variables.
- A justified unit of analysis.
- An appropriate statistical method.
- Assumptions and limitations.
- A significance and reporting approach, where applicable.
