# SkillSignal SA — Sampling Strategy

**Project ID:** SSA-001
**Document ID:** SSA-DOC-011
**Version:** 0.1
**Status:** Preliminary framework — Section B approval pending

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the framework for selecting job
advertisements for the SkillSignal SA research dataset.

The final sampling design must balance defensibility,
source availability, research coverage and the zero-budget
project constraint.

## 2. Target Population

Technology-related job advertisements relevant to employment
in South Africa during the approved 12-month research period.

The operational population definition remains pending.

## 3. Unit of Analysis

The proposed primary unit of analysis is one unique
job advertisement.

Repeated advertisements, repostings and advertisements
appearing on multiple sources require a documented
deduplication approach.

## 4. Sampling Approach

The acquisition strategy is confirmed as hybrid.

The precise combination of acquisition approaches,
source selection and sampling techniques remains pending
Sections B and C.

Possible approaches for evaluation include:
- Stratified sampling.
- Purposive source selection.
- Census of accessible records from selected sources.
- Time-based sampling.
- A documented combination of approaches.

These are options for evaluation, not approved methods.

## 5. Proposed Inclusion Criteria

Candidates for inclusion may include advertisements that:
- Relate to one of the seven approved job families.
- Are relevant to employment in South Africa.
- Fall within the approved research period.
- Contain sufficient information for at least one
  research objective.
- Were acquired through a permitted method.

Final inclusion rules require approval.

## 6. Proposed Exclusion Criteria

Candidates for exclusion may include:
- Duplicate advertisements.
- Advertisements outside the research period.
- Roles outside the approved job-family scope.
- Advertisements with insufficient usable information.
- Records acquired through prohibited or unapproved methods.
- Non-job content such as training promotions or general articles.

Final exclusion rules require approval.

## 7. Sample Size

No target dataset size has been approved.

The sample-size target must consider:
- Source availability.
- Coverage across seven job families.
- Geographic representation.
- Temporal representation.
- Data completeness.
- Analytical requirements.
- Acquisition and processing capacity.

A target must not be selected solely to make the dataset
appear large.

## 8. Representativeness and Bias

Potential limitations include:
- Overrepresentation of particular job boards.
- Repeated advertisements.
- Employer self-selection.
- Unequal source coverage across provinces.
- Limited visibility of informal recruitment.
- Missing or incomplete salary information.
- Underrepresentation of smaller employers.
- Changes in source availability over time.

The final research must disclose relevant sampling limitations.
