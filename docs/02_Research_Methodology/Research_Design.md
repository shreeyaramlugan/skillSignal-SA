# SkillSignal SA — Research Design

**Project ID:** SSA-001
**Document ID:** SSA-DOC-009
**Version:** 0.1
**Status:** Initial framework — Section B approval pending

---

## 1. Research Overview

SkillSignal SA investigates advertised technology employment
demand in South Africa.

The project aims to transform permitted job advertisement data
into structured datasets, statistical findings, skills intelligence
and interactive business intelligence reporting.

## 2. Research Purpose

The research will investigate:
- Advertised technology skills demand.
- Experience and qualification requirements.
- Graduate and junior employment opportunities.
- Geographic and temporal labour-market patterns.
- Relationships between technical skills.
- Salary transparency in technology advertisements.

## 3. Geographic Scope

South Africa.

Province, city and locality classifications will be standardised
where source information supports reliable classification.

## 4. Research Period

A 12-month research window is approved.

Exact start and end dates remain pending Section B.

## 5. Research Population

The intended population is technology-related job advertisements
relevant to the South African employment market.

The operational definition of an eligible advertisement,
including treatment of remote and international opportunities,
must be finalised in Section B.

## 6. Initial Job-Family Scope

The seven approved job families are:

1. Software development.
2. Data and analytics.
3. Cybersecurity.
4. IT infrastructure and support.
5. Cloud and DevOps.
6. Quality assurance and testing.
7. Business technology.

Final classification rules remain pending.

## 7. Research Approach

The project intends to use quantitative analysis of structured
job advertisement data, supported by text processing and
classification techniques.

Descriptive, comparative and inferential methods may be
considered where the data supports them.

No statistical method is approved by this document.

## 8. Research Outputs

Expected outputs include:
- Structured research dataset.
- Data-quality assessment.
- Skills taxonomy.
- Skills extraction results.
- Statistical findings.
- Geographic and temporal analysis.
- Interactive intelligence report.
- Research publication.

## 9. Research Integrity

The research must:
- Use permitted data sources.
- Preserve data provenance.
- Avoid fabricated or unsupported findings.
- Distinguish observed results from interpretations.
- Disclose sampling and measurement limitations.
- Document transformations and assumptions.
- Avoid presenting advertisement counts as a complete
  representation of the entire labour market.

## 10. Outstanding Decisions

Section B must resolve:
- Exact research dates.
- Research questions and hypotheses.
- Operational population definition.
- Sampling approach.
- Inclusion and exclusion criteria.
- Sample-size target and justification.
- Analytical variables.
- Statistical methodology.
