Main project objectives
1. Acquire a defensible sample of South African technology job advertisements.
2. Develop a reproducible data acquisition and engineering pipeline.
3. Clean, standardise and classify job advertisements.
4. Identify and evaluate advertised skill requirements.
5. Investigate labour-market patterns statistically.
6. Investigate temporal and geographic patterns.
7. Examine experience, qualifications and salary transparency.
8. Investigate relationships between technical skills.
9. Build an interactive intelligence report.
10. Publish evidence-based research findings.
11. Establish reproducibility and technical quality.
12. Demonstrate professional analytical competencies.
