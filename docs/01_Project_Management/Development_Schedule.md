# SkillSignal SA — Development Schedule

**Project ID:** SSA-001\
**Document:** Development Schedule\
**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Approved\
**Planned duration:** 10 weeks\
**Contingency:** Optional Weeks 11–12

## 1. Purpose

This schedule defines the sequence of activities, milestones, dependencies and deliverables for completing SkillSignal SA.

## 2. Development Schedule

| Week | Phase | Activities | Expected deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research design | Finalise research questions, hypotheses, scope and research dates | Approved research design |
| 2 | Data sourcing | Identify and assess sources, licensing, accessibility and collection methods | Approved source register and acquisition plan |
| 3 | Data acquisition | Implement initial collection process and acquire pilot dataset | Pilot dataset and acquisition logs |
| 4 | Data engineering | Develop ingestion, cleaning, deduplication and validation processes | Reproducible ETL pipeline |
| 5 | Classification and NLP | Develop job-family classification and skills extraction | Skills taxonomy and classification process |
| 6 | Exploratory analysis | Examine dataset quality, distributions and initial patterns | Exploratory analysis and quality report |
| 7 | Statistical analysis | Analyse skills demand, geography, experience, qualifications and salary transparency | Statistical results |
| 8 | Visualisation | Develop interactive Power BI report and supporting visualisations | Interactive analytical report |
| 9 | Research publication | Interpret results and prepare research report | Draft research publication |
| 10 | Finalisation | Validate findings, test reproducibility and finalise documentation | Final portfolio-ready project |

## 4. Dependencies and Approval Gates

1. Data acquisition requires approved sources and collection rules.
2. Database implementation requires an approved data model.
3. ETL development requires defined data structures and acquisition decisions.
4. NLP and classification require an initial dataset and approved taxonomy.
5. Statistical analysis requires cleaned and validated data.
6. Dashboard development requires approved analytical outputs.
7. Publication requires completed analysis and quality review.

No major implementation should proceed ahead of its necessary research, governance or technical approval.

## 5. Contingency

Weeks 11–12 are reserved for unforeseen delays, data acquisition challenges, classification improvements, additional validation, dashboard refinements and final presentation preparation.

These weeks are not included in the official ten-week baseline.

## 6. Schedule Assumptions

- Individual project ownership.
- Zero-budget implementation.
- South African labour-market focus.
- Hybrid data acquisition strategy.
- Public publication of aggregate findings and permitted materials.
- Raw data remains private where redistribution is not permitted.
- Research dates, data sources, sample size and final technical methods remain subject to approval.
