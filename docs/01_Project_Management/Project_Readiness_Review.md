## 1. Purpose

This review evaluates whether SkillSignal SA has the project foundation, documentation, development environment and governance planning required to proceed to detailed research design and subsequent implementation.

The review distinguishes between planning readiness and approval to begin specific implementation activities.

## 5. Key Readiness Risks

| Risk | Potential impact | Recommended response |
|---|---|---|
| Insufficient job advertisement data | Weak or unrepresentative findings | Conduct a pilot acquisition and assess coverage |
| Source restrictions | Collection or publication limitations | Review source terms and permissions |
| Duplicate advertisements | Inflated demand estimates | Establish and test deduplication rules |
| Inconsistent job titles | Incorrect job-family classification | Develop documented classification rules |
| Missing salary information | Limited salary analysis | Report salary transparency and missingness |
| Skills extraction errors | Misleading skill-demand findings | Validate extraction against manually reviewed records |
| Timeline pressure | Incomplete deliverables | Prioritise core outputs and use contingency where necessary |

No risk scores or final risk ratings are assigned by this review. The project risk register remains the controlling risk record.

## 6. Readiness Decision

**Decision: Conditionally Ready**

SkillSignal SA has a sufficiently established project foundation to proceed to Section B — Research Design.

The project is not yet approved for unrestricted data acquisition, full-scale implementation or publication of research findings.

The following activities may proceed:

- Finalisation of research questions and hypotheses.
- Refinement of the sampling strategy.
- Finalisation of research scope and dates.
- Evaluation of potential data sources.
- Preparation of detailed implementation decisions.

The following activities remain subject to approval:

- Full-scale job advertisement collection.
- Implementation of source-specific acquisition processes.
- Definitive statistical analysis.
- Publication of research findings.

## 7. Recommended Next Phase

The next phase is **Section B — Research Design**.

Section B should finalise:

1. Research problem and justification.
2. Research aim and objectives.
3. Research questions.
4. Research hypotheses.
5. Research scope and boundaries.
6. Research period.
7. Population and sampling strategy.
8. Variables and analytical measures.
9. Statistical methodology.
10. Research limitations and ethical considerations.

The approved research design will provide the basis for data-source selection and acquisition planning.

## 8. Review Conclusion

The project has completed its initial planning, documentation and development-environment foundations.

The ten-week development schedule provides the approved planning baseline. The optional Weeks 11–12 remain contingency time and are not part of the baseline duration.

Progression into Section B is authorised by this readiness assessment. This does not constitute approval for unrestricted data collection or full implementation.
