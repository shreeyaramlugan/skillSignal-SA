## 3. Risk Assessment

Each risk must eventually be assessed using:

- Probability: 1–5
- Severity: 1–5
- Risk score: Probability × Severity

| Score | Classification |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Moderate |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |

These are initial management thresholds.
Individual probability and severity scores must
be assigned during detailed risk reviews.

## 4. Risk Management Rules

1. Every identified risk must have a unique ID.
2. Every high or critical risk must have a mitigation.
3. Risks must be reviewed throughout the project.
4. New risks must be added when identified.
5. Closed risks must retain their historical records.
