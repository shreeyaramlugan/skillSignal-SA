
# SkillSignal SA

### South African Technology Labour-Market Intelligence

**Turning job-market signals into career intelligence.**

## Project Overview

SkillSignal SA is an individual data analytics and research
project investigating advertised technology employment demand
in South Africa.

The project aims to transform permitted job advertisement
data into structured datasets, statistical findings,
skills intelligence and interactive business intelligence
reporting.

## Project Objectives

- Investigate advertised technology skills demand.
- Analyse experience and qualification requirements.
- Examine graduate and junior employment opportunities.
- Investigate geographic and temporal patterns.
- Identify relationships between technical skills.
- Develop reproducible data engineering and analytical pipelines.
- Communicate findings through interactive reporting.

## Research Scope

- Geographic focus: South Africa
- Research window: 12 months
- Initial job families: 7
- Target duration: 8–12 weeks
- Budget: R0

## Technology Stack

Proposed technologies:

- Python
- PostgreSQL
- Pandas
- SciPy
- Statsmodels
- spaCy
- Power BI

Final technology decisions will be documented
as the project progresses.

## Research and Data Policy

The project prioritises responsible data acquisition,
data provenance, reproducibility and statistical integrity.

Restricted raw datasets, personal information,
credentials and copyrighted material will not be
published without appropriate permission.

Public outputs will focus on aggregated findings
and permitted project materials.
